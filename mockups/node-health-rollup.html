Rollup rule (worst-of)
  • Online — Salt online AND SSH ok
  • Degraded — stale, or SSH auth-failed / not verified
  • Down — offline OR SSH unreachable
  • Unknown — never seen / never probed
  • Maintenance — overrides, neutral
Why keep both signals underneath

Salt = agent phones home (push). SSH = we can reach it directly (pull). A node can be Salt-online but SSH-broken (creds rotated → ansible fails), or Salt-offline but SSH-ok (minion crashed, box still fixable). The rollup gives one read; the tooltip keeps the "why".
